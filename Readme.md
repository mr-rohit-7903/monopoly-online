## 1. General Rules (International Mode)

### Objective
Bankrupt all opponents by acquiring, renting, and trading international countries, transport hubs, and utilities. The last remaining player with cash or unmortgaged properties wins.

### Game Setup
1. **The Banker**: One player acts as the Banker to manage unowned property deeds, houses, hotels, and bank funds.
2. **Starting Cash**: Every player starts with **$25,000**.

### Movement & Salary
- **GO Salary**: Collect **$1,500** each time you pass or land on **GO**.
- **Rolling Doubles**: Roll again after completing your turn. If you roll doubles **3 times in a row**, you are immediately sent to **Jail** without collecting salary.

### Buying & Auctioning Properties
- **Landing on an Unowned Property**: You may buy the country/site from the Bank for the listed purchase price.
- **Auctions**: If you decline to buy at face value, the Banker immediately auctions the property to all players. Bidding starts at any price, and the highest bidder gets the property deed.

### Rent & Regional Monopolies
- **Paying Rent**: Land on another player's property to pay the rent listed on its deed card.
- **Regional Monopoly Bonus**: Owning **3 countries in a region group** doubles the unimproved base rent for properties in that group.
- **Mortgaged Properties**: Mortgaged properties collect **$0 rent**.

### Building Houses & Hotels (3-House Max)
- **Prerequisite**: NA
- **3-House Limit**: In International Mode, properties hold a maximum of **3 Houses** before upgrading to a **Hotel**.
- **Hotel Upgrade**: Once a property has 3 Houses, pay the house price to upgrade to **1 Hotel** (returning the 3 houses to the Bank). Maximum 1 Hotel per property.
- **Even Building & Selling**: Houses must be built and sold evenly across all properties in a region group. Buildings sell back to the Bank at **50% of original price**.

### Transport & Utility Rules
- **Transports (Roadways, Petroleum, Airways, Railways, Satellite, Waterways)**: Transports have fixed base rent rates and upgraded rent rates when multiple transport sites are owned.

### Taxes & Duties
- **Custom Duty**: Pay **$100 per owned property site** to the Bank. (not the transport sites)
- **Travelling Duty**: Pay **$50 per owned property site** to the Bank. (not the transport sites)

### Party House & Special Spaces
- **Party House**: When landing on Party House or drawing Community Chest #8, you host a party and collect **$200 from each player**.
- **Resorts**: When landing on a Resort space, pay **$200 to each player** for vacation expenses.

### Mortgaging & Unmortgaging
- **Mortgaging**: Mortgage an unbuilt property to receive its printed **Mortgage Value**. All houses and hotels in that region group must be sold back to the Bank before mortgaging any property in the set.
- **Unmortgaging**: Pay the **Mortgage Value + 10% interest** to the Bank to restore normal rent collection.

### Jail Rules
- **Entering Jail**: Land on "Go to Jail".
- **Getting Out**:
  - Pay a **$50 fine** before rolling on your turn.
  - Roll doubles on one of your next 3 turns.
  - If no doubles are rolled by turn 3, pay **$500** and move forward by the sum rolled.
- **Permitted Actions**: Players in Jail **can still collect rent, build houses/hotels, mortgage properties, and trade**.

### Trading System
- Players may trade cash and unbuilt properties at any time.
- Properties with buildings **cannot** be traded until all houses/hotels in that group are sold back to the Bank.

### Bankruptcy & Elimination
- Occurs when a player cannot pay a debt even after selling all buildings and mortgaging all properties.
- **Debt to Player**: Transfer all cash and mortgaged deeds to the creditor.
- **Debt to Bank**: Assets revert to the Bank; properties are auctioned off immediately. Player is eliminated.

## Notifications

The application must provide real-time notifications to every connected player.

Whenever a transaction occurs, all players currently in the game should immediately receive an in-app notification.

Examples:

- John paid Sarah $1,200 (Rent)
- Bank paid Mike $1,500 (GO Salary)
- Party House: You collected $200 from each player.
- Sarah purchased Japan.
- You paid $2,500 to the Bank (Insurance Premium).

Bank -> Alex      +$1,500    GO Salary
Alex -> Sarah     -$1,200    Rent
Sarah bought Japan
Mike mortgaged Canada
Bank charged Custom Duty

Notifications should:

- Appear instantly without refreshing.
- Auto-dismiss after a few seconds.
- Be stored in a notification history.
- Be synchronized across all connected devices.
- Show who initiated the action, who received it, the amount, and the reason.

Future enhancement:
- Support Expo Push Notifications so players receive alerts even when the app is in the background.
